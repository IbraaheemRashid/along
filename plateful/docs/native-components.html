Setup & membership

Preview the person’s own schedule before account and membership.

Compare the two onboarding paths

Set up my tracker. Confirm you’re 18 or older, then choose a peptide. If you set a schedule, Along shows its days and time before offering reminders. You can add a previous injection or go straight to your schedule preview.

Add a peptide later. From the peptide picker, this opens the empty plan. Along can create the tracker without inventing a schedule, requesting notification permission or adding a dose record.

Walk me through Along. The guided welcome includes the app tour, goals and optional personalisation. Returning drafts resume on the path the person chose.
