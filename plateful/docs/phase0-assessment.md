# Along — honest Phase 0 assessment

Research snapshot: 1 November 2026. **Proceed with the user-authorised prototype; do not mark market Gate A passed.** The user selected a GLP-1 progress companion after rejecting the pantry-led concept. This is an exploration of that approved direction, not a claim that research selected a winning business.

## What the evidence supports

There is qualitative evidence that some GLP-1 users pay for specialised tracking. In a [Shotsy premium discussion](https://www.reddit.com/r/Mounjaro/comments/1ro1b05/shotsy_app_paid_premium_worth_it/), commenters self-report paying, renewing and appreciating charts or the developer's work. Others see little value, use free alternatives or say they will not renew. One describes discovering the app through other people's graphs. These are self-reports from a convenience sample, not receipts, conversion rates or market sizing. Several motivations concern medication estimates, a feature outside Along's scope.

The competitive pattern is clear: [MeAgain](https://meagain.com/) combines tracking, Journey Cards and Capy in a paid bundle. [Shotsy](https://shotsyapp.com/) offers progress charts and records, while describing core tracking as free. [Finch](https://help.finchcare.com/hc/en-us/articles/42149821015693-New-User-Guide) connects daily actions to a customisable companion and offers an optional subscription. A focused experience can coexist, but a mascot plus a graph is already occupied territory. Published offers validate business-model attempts, not independently verified revenue.

Earlier community research found ChatGPT used for meal planning and ingredient substitutions. That supported a food-planning problem, not demand for a pantry-first product, and does not establish demand for Along. We have no verified repeated weekly ChatGPT prompt for this progress-companion concept. It should not be retrofitted into the repeated-prompt test.

## Three directions, scored using the playbook's exact criteria

These are subjective hypotheses, not measurements. Reference products are listed in the next table. A high frequency score means an opportunity for daily use, not demonstrated retention.

| Criterion, 1–5 | Along: personal progress companion | Dose and symptom record | AI journal reflection |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Pain or desire | 3 | 4 | 3 |
| Frequency | 5 | 4 | 4 |
| Demoability | 5 | 4 | 4 |
| AI is the engine | **1** | **1** | 2 |
| Willingness to pay | 2 | 3 | 2 |
| Reachable audience | 4 | 4 | 4 |
| Low risk | 3 | 2 | 3 |
| **Total / 35** | **23** | **22** | **22** |

| Direction | One-liner | References | Watch-out |
|---|---|---|---|
| Along, approved prototype | For adults on GLP-1 medication, a short check-in becomes a personal journey in under a minute. | MeAgain, Finch, Shotsy | Optional journaling may be pleasant without becoming worth paying for. Sensitive records remain sensitive even without advice. |
| Dose and symptom record, comparison only | For adults recording treatment, a few entries become a chronological appointment record. | Shotsy, MeAgain | Strong incumbents and free tools; medication features require stricter design and clinical boundaries. Not authorised day-one scope. |
| AI journal reflection, later possibility | For adults keeping a GLP-1 journal, their own entries become a weekly reflection. | Finch, MeAgain | Summaries alone may add little value and can introduce unsupported interpretations. AI is useful at most, not indispensable. |

Along's strongest scores are its potential frequency and visible check-in-to-journey demo. Pain scores 3 because the precise unmet job is not established. Willingness to pay scores 2 because broad competitor subscriptions do not prove people pay for this narrower offer. Reach scores 4 because communities can be identified, but the founder has no confirmed audience or distribution relationships. Risk scores 3 because mood, appetite and weight are sensitive, even with no clinical claims. AI scores **1** because the core works deterministically. Adding a model would not cure that score.

The playbook requires at least 25/35, no 1s, demand signals and sufficient acquisition economics. Along fails that exact scorecard today. The user's explicit prototype instruction permits learning through a local build; it does not change the threshold or establish a business case. The playbook's broader UX, stack and iteration methods remain useful.

## Evidence gaps that stay open

- **1–3★ review audit: incomplete.** Public App Store pages exposed complaint text, but the extraction flattened each review's star icons into the same sequence. The earlier RSS attempt returned zero usable entries, and a follow-up fetch was interrupted. We cannot label those reviews as a verified low-star cohort or quantify recurring defects. Sampled category pages included [MyFitnessPal](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/myfitnesspal-calorie-counter/id341232718?see-all=reviews), [Cal AI](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/cal-ai-calorie-tracker/id6480417616?see-all=reviews), [MeAgain](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/meagain-glp-1-tracker-app/id6744178534?see-all=reviews) and [Pep](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/glp-1-weight-loss-tracker-pep/id6504788281?see-all=reviews). This is not a fresh Along-specific audit.
- **Current grossing ranks and revenue: unverified.** Public Sensor Tower pages did not expose usable ranking data. Do not call the sampled apps verified current top-grossing leaders.
- **TikTok/Reels: unverified.** TikTok search produced access/server errors and no native comments were verified. Reddit discovery anecdotes are a different channel; they do not fulfil the TikTok/Reels comment test.
- **Trends and ads: incomplete.** No reliable Google Trends time series, Meta ad-spend data, competitor CAC, creative-performance data or Along demand metrics were established.
- **Commercial behaviour: unknown.** There is no Along waitlist conversion, activated cohort, retention result, payment, renewal or measured willingness to pay. A good-looking prototype cannot supply these.

## Unit economics at the proposed US$29.99/year

All funnel inputs below are assumptions for first-year annual subscriptions. No renewals are counted. Dollars are USD; they are not GBP prices. `gross revenue per download = trial-start rate × trial-to-paid rate × 29.99`.

| Scenario | Trial starts | Trial → paid | Gross / download | After 15% store fee | After 30% fee stress case |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Weak | 10% | 20% | $0.60 | $0.51 | $0.42 |
| Playbook assumption | 30% | 30% | $2.70 | $2.29 | $1.89 |
| Strong | 40% | 40% | $4.80 | $4.08 | $3.36 |

Apple publishes a [15% Small Business Program commission for eligible participants](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/small-business-program/); eligibility/enrolment have not been verified for this app. The 30% column is a sensitivity assumption, not a claim about every storefront or subscription year.

The deterministic local core has **zero model calls**. A hypothetical later AI recap should budget a full annual cohort: assume one introductory call per download plus 52 weekly calls for each payer. Calls per acquired download are `1 + 52 × trial-start × trial-to-paid`, or 2.04 / 5.68 / 9.32 in these scenarios. At an assumed all-in $0.01 per call, annual cohort AI expense is $0.020 / $0.057 / $0.093 per download; at $0.10 it is $0.204 / $0.568 / $0.932. These are cost sensitivities, not verified model quotations. At the base funnel and high AI cost, $2.29 becomes about $1.73 before other costs.

Also subtract taxes as applicable, refunds, payment/paywall tooling, hosting, analytics, support, content/artwork, developer accounts and marketing. [Cloudflare Workers Paid starts at $5/month plus relevant usage](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers/platform/pricing/); no paid service has been purchased for this prototype. Fixed costs matter most when there are few paying customers. Creator payments and founder editing time count as acquisition costs. The gross $2.70 benchmark is therefore **not** a safe cost-per-download budget or profit estimate. A waitlist signup is neither a download nor a paying subscriber.

**Next decision:** test whether people repeatedly use the progress record and prefer it enough to pay. Continue only from measured behaviour, while retaining the user-approved scope. Do not add medical features or AI merely to improve a score.
