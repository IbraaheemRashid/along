# Along — one-week validation kit

Prepared 1 November 2026. **Draft for a future human-run test.** No videos, outreach, ads or waitlist have been published by this research task. No budget or production deployment has been authorised. The local prototype is a research artefact, not market validation.

## Test the actual promise

Hypothesis: some adults on GLP-1 medication want a brief daily ritual that creates a personal progress record, and will return for that record. The paid hypothesis is separate: enough of them would subscribe at US$29.99/year for a sustainably maintained product.

Do not test a pantry planner, medication calculator or automatic nutrition coach under Along's name. Use sample entries, visibly marked as fictional, in all demos. No body transformations, manufactured customer quotes, made-up retention numbers or health outcomes.

## Ten short-form hooks

Each video can be 12–20 seconds: show the result in the first two seconds, demonstrate one real action, and end with “Along prototype — join the early-access list.” Record actual UI rather than implying unfinished screens work. “Under a minute” is a target until timed.

| # | Opening hook | What the demo shows | What it tests |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | “Your GLP-1 journey, one small entry at a time.” | Today's check-in becomes a dated journey card. | Core promise comprehension. |
| 2 | “A place for the moments you want to remember.” | Add a user-written milestone; find it again in the timeline. | Value beyond daily logging. |
| 3 | “What did your week actually look like?” | Seven days of fictional entries become a factual recap. | Recap interest. |
| 4 | “You can leave weight out of your check-in.” | Complete a check-in with no weight entry. | Appeal of optional weight tracking. |
| 5 | “Missed a few days? Start with today.” | Return after a visible gap; no lost accessory or penalty. | Response to gentle re-entry. |
| 6 | “Meet Pip. Make this little space yours.” | Change Pip's accessory after a check-in unlock. | Character appeal, without confusing it with paid demand. |
| 7 | “A note to your future self.” | Write one sentence and revisit it in the journey. | Whether memory, rather than charts, is the useful job. |
| 8 | “Your routines, in your words.” | Choose a routine and record what happened today. | Relevance of personalisation. |
| 9 | “A weight record, when you want one.” | Add an optional value, switch kg/lb, view a neutral trend. | Chart interest and unit usability. |
| 10 | “Could this become your daily GLP-1 check-in?” | Full short loop from home to saved entry to recap. | Clear intent to try the actual product. |

Use the same factual end card and track each video's source separately. Never ask users to provide medication doses, weight or health histories in public comments. Do not use a mascot's distress as a hook.

## Honest waitlist copy

**Headline:** A small daily check-in. Your journey, remembered.

**Body:** Along is a progress companion being built for adults using GLP-1 medication. Record how you feel, keep your chosen routines and personal milestones, and look back on your week with Pip.

**Disclosure:** Prototype in development. Screens use fictional sample entries. Along does not provide medical or medication advice.

**Price research text:** We are exploring a US$29.99 yearly subscription. Joining the list is free and does not start a trial or subscription. Local pricing is undecided.

**CTA:** Join the early-access list.

Collect email and consent for early-access updates, plus an optional “What would you use this for?” answer. Avoid health details. Explain who operates the list and how to leave/delete the signup. Do not send marketing until a legitimate signup and suitable notice exist. Worker + D1 implementation should deduplicate signups, record source attribution and distinguish demo submissions from live ones. Local success messages are not evidence of a real signup.

## Week plan

| Day | Human-led activity | Record |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Review actual demo and privacy copy; prepare permitted accounts and test the waitlist once it is legitimately hosted. Recruit five adult volunteers through allowed channels. | Baseline zero; exclude team and test visits. |
| 2 | Publish hooks 1 and 4; run two short usability sessions. | Views, qualified visitors, unique signups, task completion. |
| 3 | Publish hooks 2 and 6; run remaining sessions. | Which promise people repeat back; where they hesitate. |
| 4 | Publish hooks 3 and 5; invite consenting testers to use their own empty journey. | Activation and whether they return voluntarily. |
| 5 | Publish hooks 7 and 8. Ask what app, note or spreadsheet they currently use and what they pay for. | Concrete current behaviour and switching objections. |
| 6 | Publish hooks 9 and 10. Show the proposed price after demonstrating value. | Price reaction and willingness to join a later paid pilot; verbal interest is not a purchase. |
| 7 | Review the full funnel and interview notes; arrange a follow-up when each tester reaches day seven. | Decide continue, refine or pause commercial work. Do not label incomplete cohorts D7. |

## Decision rules, set before posting

- The playbook's signal is **more than 10%** of genuine landing visitors joining, plus saves/shares or unsolicited app-interest comments. Report numerator and denominator; seek at least 100 relevant unique visitors as a practical initial sample, not statistical proof. Ten signups from 100 visitors is exactly 10%, not above it.
- Inspect whether curiosity is about the record or only the mascot. A popular clip without qualified visits and use is weak product evidence.
- In five sessions, aim for at least four people to finish the first check-in unaided within one minute and retrieve it. This is a small usability screen, not market validation.
- For an initial ten-person pilot, a proposed exploratory retention signal is six people using the app on three distinct days in their first seven. Report voluntary use separately from study reminders; do not call this an industry benchmark.
- Record actual payment and refund behaviour only in a separately authorised real paid test. Stated willingness, emails and mock paywalls cannot measure trial conversion.
- Track all creator spend and production time. Cost per qualified visit, signup and activated user are different metrics. Later calculate acquisition cost per payer and compare with conservative net first-year value.

Even good results do not remove the playbook's AI-engine score of 1. They could justify pursuing a non-AI companion business explicitly; they cannot retroactively claim the exact AI niche gate was satisfied.
