# Along — references and evidence limits

Primary pages inspected 1 November 2026. These are product-pattern references; no assets, characters, logos or proprietary copy should be reproduced. The current review is of public product descriptions, not a hands-on audit of paid onboarding or every screen.

## MeAgain — direct category reference

[Official product page](https://meagain.com/). Markets a combined GLP-1 tracker, Journey Cards and Capy; lists a Premium subscription. The overlap is material: progress and a companion are not novel. Study how one product gives a journey a coherent home; Along must test whether its smaller scope is preferable. Company testimonials and audience claims are not independent revenue verification. Do not borrow the character or medical-estimate features.

## Shotsy — records and charts

[Official product page](https://shotsyapp.com/). Describes shot/side-effect records, progress charts and PDF export; says core tracking is free, with premium tools. Study readable longitudinal records and user-controlled export. This also raises a commercial challenge: charging for a narrower record requires clear value. Along's chart should describe entered measurements, without medication calculations or clinical interpretation.

## Finch — daily ritual and customisation

[Official new-user guide](https://help.finchcare.com/hc/en-us/articles/42149821015693-New-User-Guide), updated 22 December 2025. Describes goals, adventures, outfits, reflections and an optional Plus subscription. Study a clear small action followed by a visible result. Along uses an original Pip design and cumulative accessories; it does not copy Finch's character, economy or streak implementation. [Finch's billing help](https://help.finchcare.com/hc/en-us/categories/37934263294477-Subscriptions-Billing) confirms subscription support exists, not the size of paid demand.

## One reference per main surface

| Along surface | Main reference pattern | Along-specific design choice |
|---|---|---|
| Welcome and first action | Finch's small initial goals | Short onboarding, optional name and routines; immediate local value. |
| Today and Pip | Finch's visible companion loop | One primary check-in action; neutral welcome after an absence. |
| Journey and milestones | MeAgain's journey framing | Date-led personal records with clear sample provenance. |
| Optional weight trend | Shotsy's progress charts | Neutral direction, kg/lb display, no prediction or target judgement. |
| Weekly recap | MeAgain's progress framing | Deterministic facts with record counts and user-written milestones. |
| Data controls | Shotsy's export pattern | Explicit local export/delete and honest backup status. |

Full reference screenshots, App Store Search API ranking, Mobbin/Screens Design flows and side-by-side visual comparison remain pending. Do not mark the playbook's complete reference-UI checklist done on the basis of this text review.

It is evidence that payment motivations differ, not audited revenue or Along willingness to pay. The visible page used relative dates; no exact posting date is inferred here. User medication opinions are not adopted as health guidance.

The parent research also verified an app-discovery question in [this r/Zepbound discussion](https://www.reddit.com/r/Zepbound/comments/1dmng2b/). A later fetch returned a cache miss.
